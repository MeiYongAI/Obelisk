Here you can manage your equipment, as well as modify them using Upgrades, Charms and Stat Fusion. Modifications all require that the equipment is soulbound first.

Upgrading equipment will increase the number of Charm Points available, and adds bonues relative to its base stats. The maximum number of upgrades for an equipment is capped by the number of cleared Item Worlds.

Attaching Charms to your equipment can improve or add new stats, or add special effects and various other boons. Charms and Charm Pouches can be obtained by offering trophies in The Shrine, or purchased from other players in The Market.

Stat Fusion lets you improve Legendary+ equipment by sacrificing another Legendary+ equipment together with various materials to increase its base stats. (Persistent Only)

Materials for upgrades and stat fusion can be obtained from salvaging unwanted equipment or raising monsters in the Monster Lab, or purchased from other players in The Market.

Select an equipment first to show the available options.
